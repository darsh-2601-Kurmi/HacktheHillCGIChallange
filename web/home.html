One complaint. One case. One screen.

Northwind supplies water and electricity. OneCase gives every complaint, from any channel, one case that keeps its history from first contact to close.

Find Out More Open the agent desk

History gets lost at every handover

Complaints are passed from one system to another, and every handover loses the history. OneCase opens one case from any channel and keeps it to the end.

See what it does

What OneCase does

One intake

Phone, web form, email or the customer page: every contact opens the same kind of case.

Full history

Every step stays on the case, so the customer never has to explain twice.

Bill check

Compares each bill with the meter reads and corrects estimates when a smart read exists.

Right team first

Plain routing rules send the case to the team that can close it.
