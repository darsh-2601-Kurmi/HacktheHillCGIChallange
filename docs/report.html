Finding 1 · The score

The regulator score follows days to close

The score fell every single month, from 4.30 to 2.58, while average days to close rose from 9.1 to 38.2. A straight line fits the two closely (r = −0.98), so reaching 4.0 means closing complaints in about 19.0 days.

Finding 2 · Transfers

Whether a complaint gets transferred depends on the system that took it in

34.9% of complaints were passed from one system to another. Split by category, channel, priority or quarter, that rate barely moves (33–37%). Split by the system that took the complaint in, it is 0% for CaseTrack and 46–47% for the other three.

Finding 3 · Billing and meters

Billing and metering drive the volume, and billing still ignores the smart meters

63% of complaints are about bills and meter reads, and 59% of billing complaints end with the bill corrected or refunded. In the four smart-meter regions, smart meters went from 30% to 81% of homes, yet the share of bills on an estimate did not fall (21% → 23%).

Finding 4 · Answers and the AI pilot

A quarter of complaints only needed an answer, and the AI pilot made things worse

5,865 complaints (23.1%) could have been closed with information alone. They still took 28.0 days and were transferred as often as any other (34.4%). The 2025 AI assistant pilot, meant to answer these, got worse every month: fewer sessions contained, more customers coming back.

Finding 5 · Staffing

Calderfield lost half its agents, and its results match every other region

Calderfield went from 73 agents to 37, most of it in March 2026. If staffing drove the decline, Calderfield would stand out after March. It doesn't: complaints opened since March take 33.5–34.8 days to close in every region, Calderfield 33.8.

Finding 6 · Reaching 4.0

Per-case fixes alone don't reach 4.0; clearing the backlog does

The scenario engine projects the next 12 months two ways. The plan method subtracts per-case savings from today's days to close: even every fix at maximum reaches only 3.41. The backlog-aware view also lets days fall as the open backlog clears, the way days have tracked the backlog for 24 months: the recommended package reaches 4.0 in month 5.
